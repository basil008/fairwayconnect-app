# FairwayConnect
## Golf Society Management Platform — User Guide & Feedback Request

---

# Welcome!

**FairwayConnect** is a mobile-first web app designed to make running a golf society effortless. From event sign-ups to live scoring and automatic prize calculations — everything in one place.

**We'd love your feedback** on the key features. Please try them out and let us know what works, what doesn't, and what you'd like to see added.

---

# Getting Started

## Access the App

🔗 **Live App:** [fairwayconnect.fly.dev](https://fairwayconnect.fly.dev)

Works on any device — phone, tablet, or laptop. No app download required.

## Demo Society: Oscar Golf Society

The app is pre-loaded with a demo event: **Spring Classic at Donabate Golf Club** with 40 members, tee times, and live scoring data so you can explore all features immediately.

# Key Features

## 1. Society Home Dashboard
**Route:** `/`

- Event countdown timer
- Quick links to all sections
- At-a-glance society info

**Feedback Question:** *Is the home screen layout intuitive? What would you want to see first?*

---

## 2. Members Directory
**Route:** `/members`

- Full member list with handicaps
- RSVP status for upcoming events (✅ Going / ❌ Not Going / ⏳ Pending)
- Search and filter members

**Feedback Question:** *What member info is most useful? Should we show historical stats?*

---

## 3. Event Details & RSVP
**Route:** `/event`

- Event date, venue, format details
- Tee time allocations with playing partners
- One-tap RSVP

**Feedback Question:** *Is the tee time display clear? How do you currently manage RSVPs?*

---

## 4. Live Scorecard Entry
**Route:** `/scoring`

- Hole-by-hole score entry
- **Auto Stableford calculation** based on handicap and stroke index
- Works offline — scores save locally and sync when back online
- Simple tap interface designed for on-course use

**Feedback Question:** *How easy is the scoring interface? Would you use this during a round?*

---

## 5. Live Leaderboard
**Route:** `/leaderboard`

- Real-time leaderboard (auto-refreshes every 10 seconds)
- Shows current position, points, and holes completed
- Highlights players still on course vs finished

**Feedback Question:** *Is live scoring valuable to your society? How often do you check leaderboards during events?*

---

## 6. Results & Prizes
**Route:** `/results`

- Final standings after event completion
- **R&A-standard countback** for tied positions
- Side competition winners (Nearest the Pin, Longest Drive)
- Prize breakdown

**Feedback Question:** *What prizes/competitions does your society run? Any we should add?*

---

## 7. Admin Dashboard
**Route:** `/admin`

- Event management
- Member management
- Score verification
- Finalise results

**Feedback Question:** *What admin tasks take the most time currently? What would save you effort?*

# How It Works

## Before the Event
1. **Create event** — Set date, venue, format, tee times
2. **Members RSVP** — One tap to confirm attendance
3. **Tee times assigned** — Automatic or manual groupings

## During the Event
4. **Live scoring** — Each player enters scores hole-by-hole
5. **Leaderboard updates** — Everyone can follow the action in real-time

## After the Event
6. **Results calculated** — Automatic Stableford points + countback
7. **Prizes announced** — Winners displayed with full breakdown
