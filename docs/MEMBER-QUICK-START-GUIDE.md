# 🏌️ FairwayConnect Quick Start Guide
## Aer Lingus Golf Society

---

## 📱 **3 Easy Steps to Get Started**

### **1️⃣ Open Your Link**
Tap the personal link sent to you via email or WhatsApp.

### **2️⃣ Enter Your PIN**
When prompted, enter your unique 4-digit PIN.

### **3️⃣ Add to Home Screen** *(Recommended)*

**iPhone:**
1. Tap **Share** button (□ with ↑)
2. Scroll and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**

**Android:**
1. Tap **menu** (⋮) in corner
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"** or **"Install"**

---

## ✨ **What's Inside**

### 🏠 **Home**
- View your upcoming tee time
- See who you're playing with
- Check last event results

### ⛳ **Live Board**
- Real-time scores during events
- Current standings
- Front 9 / Back 9 leaders

### 📊 **GOTY (Order of Merit)**
- Season standings
- Your current position
- Deductions tracker

### 📅 **Calendar**
- Upcoming events
- Venue information
- Entry fees

### ✍️ **Scoring** *(During Events)*
- Submit your scorecard
- Hole-by-hole entry
- Auto-calculates Stableford points

### 🏆 **Results**
- Event winners
- Prize allocations
- Historical results
