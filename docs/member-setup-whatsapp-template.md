## 🎯 **Sending Options**

### **Option A: Individual Messages**
1. Export CSV from Admin → Members
2. Send personalized WhatsApp to each member
3. Use template above with their link/PIN

### **Option B: Broadcast List**
1. Create WhatsApp broadcast list
2. Send one message with instructions
3. Attach full CSV or share list in message

### **Option C: Email Then WhatsApp Reminder**
1. Send detailed email first (setup instructions)
2. Follow up with short WhatsApp: "Check your email for ALGS app access!"
