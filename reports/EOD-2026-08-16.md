### 4. Critical Bug Fixes — Captain's/President's Prize Logic

**Two bugs discovered during testing (22:30 - 23:00):**

#### Bug 1: Deduction Exemption Not Applied (commit b9d2f7a)
- **Issue:** Captain's/President's Prize winners were being deducted -3/-2/-2 shots
- **Root cause:** Lines 453-475 of `/api/finalise/route.ts` applied deductions to special events
- **Fix:** Changed to `deduction = 0` for eventType 'captains' or 'presidents'
- **Rationale:** Per ALGS rules, prestigious prize winners are exempt from handicap deductions

#### Bug 2: Same Player Winning 1st AND 2nd (commit f7c40de)
- **Issue:** Pat O'Dwyer appearing in BOTH 1st Overall (37pts) and 2nd Overall (35pts)
- **Root cause:** 
  - 1st calculated from `sortedByRaw[0]` (raw points)
  - 2nd calculated from `sorted[1]` (adjusted points)
  - If same player topped both lists → won both prizes
- **Fix:** Filter `sorted` array to exclude 1st place winner before awarding 2nd
- **Impact:** Affected all Captain's/President's Prize events
