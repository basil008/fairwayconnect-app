# 🚪 **MEMBER LOGOUT FUNCTIONALITY ADDED**

## ✅ **How to Logout as a Member:**

### **🏠 From Member Home Page:**
1. **Look at the top right** of the home page
2. **Click the door icon** 🚪 (replaced the settings gear)
3. **Automatically returns** to FairwayConnect landing page

### **📅 From Calendar Page:**
1. **Look at the top right** of the calendar page  
2. **Click the door icon** 🚪 next to the "Season Calendar" title
3. **Automatically returns** to FairwayConnect landing page

### **🔄 From Any Other Page:**
1. **Navigate to Home** using the bottom navigation 🏠
2. **Click the door icon** 🚪 in the top right
3. **Automatically returns** to FairwayConnect landing page

## 🧹 **What Logout Clears:**
- ✅ **Member authentication** session
- ✅ **Member PIN** data
- ✅ **Guest mode** (if applicable)
- ✅ **Member identity** (name, handicap, ID)
- ✅ **Remember me** preferences
- ✅ **All stored session data**

## 🎯 **After Logout:**
- **Returns to:** Green ALGS landing page with PIN entry
- **Can re-login:** Enter member PIN or admin PIN again
- **Guest access:** Available via "Continue as Guest"
- **Clean slate:** No cached authentication data

## 💡 **Visual Indicator:**
- **Door icon** 🚪 in top right corner
- **Hover effect:** Icon gets darker on hover
- **Tooltip:** Shows "Logout" on hover
- **One-click logout:** No confirmation needed
